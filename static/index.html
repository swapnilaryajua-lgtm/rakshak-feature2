"Why This Is Safe" & Adaptive Defense Trade-Off

Judges' defense review: why CyberSentinel balances attack containment against benign traffic preservation, and how the safeguards eliminate collateral damage.

1. Minimum 2 Suspicious Flows

A single anomaly alert is weak evidence (5% benign false-alarm rate). Requiring 2 suspicious flows within a sliding window prevents single-flow anomaly noise from causing accidental firewall blocks.

2. Bounded TTL with Continuous Renewal

Policies expire automatically after 30 records unless active hostile traffic persists. When the attacker continues flooding, renewals seamlessly extend protection without locking out legitimate addresses indefinitely.

3. Strict Causality (Trigger Stays ALLOW)

Per firewall causality, a policy is activated strictly for later records. The triggering packet itself stays ALLOW because it occurred before the rule was actuated in the ledger.

4. High Benign-Share Protection

IPs with > 25% benign traffic in their temporal window are protected from whole-IP blocking unless overwhelming attack volumes (≥ 6 attacks) are detected.
